How to use

Choose files and channels. Select a region in the overview or enter exact start/end times and choose Apply time window. Previous/Next move by one window.

Top-plot zoom is temporary; exports use the selected window and visible recordings. Select files with their checkboxes; legend clicks only change the current plot.

No filtering, baseline correction, normalisation or resampling is added.

Continuous EDF and EDF+C are supported. EDF+D and BDF are unsupported.
